Does GYM BRO'S make its own clothes?

No. We're a retailer. Every product is made by the brand shown on its listing, and we buy directly from those brands.

How do the clothes fit?

Fit depends on the brand. Each listing notes whether it runs true to size or oversized. When in doubt, size up on oversized cuts.

Is the protein really organic?

Yes. Both proteins we stock are certified organic, sweetened only with organic cane sugar or stevia.

How should I take creatine?

Most people take one 5g scoop a day mixed into water, a shake or coffee. Timing matters less than consistency. Check with your doctor if you have a medical condition.
