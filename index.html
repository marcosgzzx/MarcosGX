The Essential Tee is a true-to-size athletic fit. The Pump Cover is oversized — size down if you want it closer to the body.

How should I take creatine?

Most people take one 5g scoop a day mixed into water, a shake or coffee. Timing matters less than consistency. Check with your doctor if you have a medical condition.
